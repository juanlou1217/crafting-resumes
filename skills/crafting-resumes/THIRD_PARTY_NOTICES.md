## Modification summary

The upstream concepts were selectively rewritten and reorganized for a truthful,
evidence-first Chinese job-search workflow. Terminology, routing, interview rules,
review dimensions, templates, and verification behavior were adapted to this
Skill's requirements rather than copied as a complete upstream workflow.

PDF delivery was changed to a native Obsidian export workflow. The
Easy-Job-Tutor HTML builder, themes, screenshots, and real-person examples were
not adapted or included; only the listed verification and layout concepts were
used.
